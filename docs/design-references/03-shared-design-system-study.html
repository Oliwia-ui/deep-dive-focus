Assignment artifact · style + interaction study

One foundation, two distinct worlds.

QuestLog and Deep Dive share dependable interaction rules, spacing, accessibility, and component logic. Their skins communicate different emotional jobs: thoughtful planning in an enchanted archive, and calm concentration through an underwater instrument.

What this study decided

  • Keep control dimensions and state logic shared.
  • Let surface shape, color, and motion carry personality.
  • Use familiar labels beside playful app language.
  • Prefer tactile, purposeful feedback over decoration.

Shared foundation

Reuse the invisible quality.

  • 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 spacing rhythm
  • 44px minimum interactive targets
  • Primary / secondary / quiet action hierarchy
  • Explicit loading, success, warning, error, and empty states
  • Keyboard focus and reduced-motion support

Final design position

Personality belongs in the skin—not the rules.

QuestLog can feel storied and tactile while Deep Dive feels calm and measured. Both remain legible, reliable, and straightforward enough to assess as coursework and to use every day.
